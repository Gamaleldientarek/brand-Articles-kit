# Article workflow

For the complete staged ClickUp process, read [Idea to ready for design](clickup-workflow.md). It defines the research/plan outputs, transition criteria, field handling and verified handoff. For the detailed search and source-audit method, read [research.md](research.md).

## Scope and decision
Read the card or supplied brief, original draft/report and current document tabs. Identify source language(s), existing edition(s), author, brand and requested stage. Do not assume a linked PDF is a bilingual article.

Select a long Article when the argument needs research, comparison and practical explanation. Use a focused Blog Post for a narrower authored account. Long-form default: 2,000–2,500 body words per requested edition, excluding headings, byline, citations, SEO metadata and briefs. Preserve an explicit user length.

State the angle, reader and desired takeaway. Build an outline around distinct questions rather than a fixed number of sections. When research is requested, follow research.md; for search intent follow seo.md. Do not force unrelated inventory keywords into the argument.

## Draft
Use a concrete opening that earns the topic. Keep examples connected where useful. Label hypothetical scenarios once clearly and whenever ambiguity returns. Cite externally verifiable claims near the claim, using direct sources. Never reproduce report errors just because a download CTA points to it.

Write Arabic first for a full package unless asked otherwise. Write an English adaptation with equivalent meaning, limitations and relevant keywords. Do not invent an English report if only an Arabic PDF exists.

## Review
Check argument, repetition, unsupported claims, terminology, brand voice and length. Review the title after the body is finished. A proposed workflow is not a measured result. A correlation is not causal ROI. Account for implementation, migration and maintenance in financial discussions.

For authored content, preserve the actual voice and experience. Flag material new claims for author review without inventing “approval”.

## Designer handoff
Include a 3–5-sentence Arabic summary (default four), roughly 45–75 words: topic, problem, example or approach, central takeaway. It is for a designer or colleague, not an SEO description.

Use visual-briefs.md for the default two images. Cover earns attention; internal image explains a specific section. Keep briefs outside the article body.

## Full package
- Arabic and English drafts as requested.
- Titles, SEO metadata and keyword plan.
- Separate Thumbnail Brief and Image Brief.
- Source/review notes when material.
- Card summary and article link when remote handoff is requested.

Use delivery.md for native document tabs and fields. A corrected article does not mean the source PDF was repaired. Record a source-report blocker in the review tab and retain that dependency when simplifying a card.
