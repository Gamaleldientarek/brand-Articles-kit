## Identity

A bilingual UX research lab serving Saudi Arabia and the wider region. It is part of the AZM X ecosystem, with its own voice and visual system. Wordmark: colab. (lowercase, one l, final period). Never “Collab”.

Internal positioning is “the leading UX lab in the region”. That is a positioning line, not a verified public claim; do not print it as fact without support.

## Voice: the empathetic analyst

Precision of a data scientist, clarity of a good storyteller. Rigorous going into the data, simple coming out. The voice exists to turn evidence into a decision.

| Dimension | Position | Aim for | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Formality | Adaptable | Formal and precise in reports; conversational in workshops | Stiff, vague, overly casual |
| Technicality | Translates complexity | Rigorous, data-driven, actionable | Superficial, abstract, confusing |
| Attitude | Confident in the evidence | Bold in insights, neutral in facts, curious, empathetic | Arrogant, biased, subjective |
| Purpose | Balances vision and action | Evidence-driven for findings; action-oriented for recommendations | Recommendations dressed as findings |

Explain what a person tried to do, where they struggled, what was observed and what should be tested next. Adjust detail to the reader; do not turn every piece into enterprise transformation language.

The upstream calibration samples include percentages (for example “85% of users”). They are tone samples, not data. Never reuse their numbers.

## Evidence discipline

Separate:
- observation from interpretation;
- automated checks from participant research;
- task completion from understanding or ease;
- an illustrative scenario from an actual study.

Use actual samples, conditions and limitations when reporting research. If there are no participant data, write a proposed test or a hypothetical example, not a finding. Avoid “users hate” without evidence, fabricated testimonials and universal conclusions from a small sample.

## Visual-brief baseline

For written briefs only:
- Dark surfaces: Deep Jade #011E14 or Pine Green #103A21, with white text. One Electric Green #34FF67 accent on dark.
- Electric Green is never a surface behind body text. Where a short Electric Green flood carries display text, that text is Deep Jade, never white.
- On white or light grounds the accent is Pine or Olive Green. Electric Green and Jade Green never appear on light grounds, in any role.
- Type: Inter for English, Alexandria for Arabic. Nothing else; legacy fonts in older files (Poppins, Sora and others) are not brand fonts.
- Numerals stay left-to-right in Arabic layouts; ranges use an ASCII hyphen.
- The pixel/dither field belongs on covers, dividers and closings, away from text and never inside explanatory charts. Its use on content slides is an open client question; keep any content-page field shallow and at an edge.
- Colab also has an A4 pixel/dither poster system; for a poster brief, point the designer to colab-design.
- Do not import AZMX blue, chevrons or fonts.
