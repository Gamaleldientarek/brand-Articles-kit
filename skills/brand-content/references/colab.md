# Colab editorial profile

Canonical brand skill: **colab-design**.
Repository: [Gamaleldientarek/colab-design](https://github.com/Gamaleldientarek/colab-design).
Entrypoint: [colab-design/SKILL.md](https://github.com/Gamaleldientarek/colab-design/blob/main/SKILL.md).
Load that skill for current Colab identity and visual rules; use its references and the documented Colab voice for the requested channel. This file is a scoped editorial extract; see the main skill's dependency-resolution guidance.

Identity: a bilingual UX research lab serving Saudi Arabia and the wider region. It is part of the AZM X ecosystem, with its own voice and visual system. Wordmark: colab. (lowercase, one l, final period).

Voice: the empathetic analyst. Curious and precise about evidence; clear and useful about its implications. Explain what a person tried to do, where they struggled, what was observed and what should be tested next.

Audience: product/UX leaders and researchers seeking reliable evidence for decisions. Adjust detail to the reader. Do not turn every article into enterprise transformation language.

Separate:
- observation from interpretation;
- automated checks from participant research;
- task completion from understanding or ease;
- an illustrative scenario from an actual study.

Use actual samples, conditions and limitations when reporting research. If there are no participant data, write a proposed test or a hypothetical example, not a finding. Avoid “users hate” without evidence, fabricated testimonials and universal conclusions from a small sample.

Visual-brief baseline: Deep Jade #011E14 or Pine Green #103A21 dark surfaces; white text; one Electric Green #34FF67 accent on dark. On white use Pine/Olive, never Electric or Jade Green. Inter for English, Alexandria for Arabic. A bounded pixel/dither field may support a cover, away from text; no motif inside explanatory charts. Do not import AZMX blue, chevrons or fonts.

Use the current complete colab-design skill for actual artwork. This extract supports writing and written visual direction, not unverified design production.
