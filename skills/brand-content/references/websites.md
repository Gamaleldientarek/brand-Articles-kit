# Website copy

This mode writes content; it does not build or deploy a website unless separately requested.

Identify page type (home, service, about, landing page or related content), brand, primary audience, visitor intent and action. Inspect the actual existing page when referenced. Preserve working structure and copy outside the requested scope.

Plan the page around the visitor's questions:
- What is this, and is it relevant to me?
- What problem does it address?
- What happens in practice?
- What evidence supports it?
- What can I do next?

These are decision criteria, not mandatory section headings. Use only sections the content justifies. Never fabricate logos, testimonials, case studies, statistics, team credentials or capabilities to fill a proof section. A neutral explanation may replace absent proof; material missing facts belong in a separate editorial note.

Deliver per requested page: H1, section headings and finished body copy, CTA/button labels, relevant microcopy, SEO title/description and proposed slug when search-oriented. Map CTA labels to known destinations or clearly record an unresolved destination outside the reader-facing text. Do not present an unverified route as live.

Read seo.md for keyword clusters and intent. Avoid competing service pages targeting the same intent without a reason. FAQs should answer actual relevant questions, not be added only to increase word count or suggest rich-result eligibility.

Arabic is written for Arabic readers, with English tool/product names retained. English is independently edited. Preserve factual equivalence while adapting hierarchy or wording. Keep locale-specific URLs/canonicals/hreflang as publication recommendations until actually implemented.

For layouts, use concise modular text with clear heading roles and practical button labels. Provide designer context only where useful. A multi-page request needs all requested pages; do not silently stop after the homepage.
