# Article handoff structure

Use only for a complete article package. Replace instructions with finished content; do not paste this template into a live card unchanged.

## Card
Arabic summary in four short sentences.
Current article document link.
Thumbnail Brief: idea, composition, copy, brand, placement and alt text.
Image Brief: distinct explanatory idea, actual section, diagram/copy and alt text.

## Document
Arabic: finished article and inline source links.
English: adapted article and inline source links.
SEO Plan: keyword provenance, cluster, placement, metadata, links and measurement.
Thumbnail Brief: standalone production instructions.
Image Brief: standalone production instructions.
Research & Review: conditional source audit and publication dependencies.
