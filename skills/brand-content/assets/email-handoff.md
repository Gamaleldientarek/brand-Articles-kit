# Email handoff structure

Choose individual email, newsletter or sequence first.

Deliver subject and finished body. Add a preheader for a campaign/newsletter.
Include the verified sender/sign-off and an actual next step when relevant.
For a sequence, give each message a distinct purpose and complete copy.
Keep editorial questions and missing commercial facts outside the sendable text.
Do not include invented recipients, dates, promises or links.
A draft is not a send confirmation.
